How to usethe controls, and reading the board

Controls

Chord
Type a chord and the shape you are holding slides to it. It stays on that chord while you change shape or strings.
Position
Moves the shape up and down the neck. The chord name follows it.
Up the neck
Steps through every place that chord lives, starting nearest the nut.
String
Slides the grip sideways. In fifths tuning that transposes by a fifth with your fingers where they are.
Play
Which strings you fret, labelled by degree. The one marked economy is the pared-down grip.
Strum
Sounds the chord. Tapping any dot sounds that note.

You can also drag the shape on the board, or use the arrow keys: left and right along the neck, up and down across the strings.

Reading the board

Filled
The notes you are playing. The brass one is the root.
Faded
The rest of the chord: where those notes sit if you want them.
Hollow
The root, when the grip does not sound it.
Open
At the nut: that open string belongs to the chord and may ring.
Muted
At the nut: stop that string, its open note is not in the chord.

Numbers are scale degrees. 1 is the root, 3 the third, 5 the fifth, and a flat sign lowers it. Switch to note names under Display.
