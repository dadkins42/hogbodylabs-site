Burin Studio is where you put a mix, a rehearsal track, or a rough demo and send it to the people who need to hear it. They click, they listen, and they leave feedback pinned to the exact second — no app to install, no account to press play.

How it works

Three steps, and the person on the other end doesn't need an account for any of it.

1

Add your tracks

Drop mixes or rehearsal audio into a folder — from the web, or the Mac and iOS apps.

2

Share the link

Send a folder's link — listen-only, or collaborate so they can leave notes.

3

Get feedback

They listen, compare takes, and comment on the exact spot. You get an email when they do.

What's inside

A review tool for the people you share with, and a practice tool for the players — in the same place.

▶️

Player with a real counter

Play, scrub, and read the exact time. Type a time like 2:34 and jump straight there.

🎚️

Practice & Reference modes

Practice adds speed and key changes to woodshed a part. Reference is clean, accurate playback for critical listening.

🗂️

Versions

Upload another take as a version of the same mix and A/B them at the same spot to hear what changed.

💬

Timestamped comments

Pin feedback to an exact moment — “push the downbeat at 1:42.” Reply and mark things resolved.

📍

Locations

Drop numbered markers and loop a section, like a DAW's memory locations — for getting around, not commenting.

🔗

Link sharing

One link to listen, another to collaborate. Share a whole folder or a single track — nothing to install.

📶

Save offline

Pull a folder onto your phone and play it with no signal — handy in the rehearsal-room dead zone.

🍎

Native Mac & iOS apps

Keyboard shortcuts, hi-res audio, record straight in — with the same library as the web.

Do I need an account?

No — not to listen, and not to comment.

Anyone you send a link to can open it in a browser and play right away. On a collaborate link they can also leave comments — they're just asked for a name so you know who's talking. You only make an account if you want your own space.

What a free account gets you

  • Your own library to upload and organize your tracks
  • Share your own folders by link — listen or collaborate
  • Keep folders other people shared with you
  • Versions, practice tools, and offline playback

Signing up is an email and a one-time code — no password to remember.

Is my stuff private?

Short version: yes. We don't sell your data, there are no ads, and there's no third-party tracking.

  • Your email is used only to sign you in — nothing else. We don't sell or rent it.
  • Your audio and comments are yours. We store them so the app works, and they're private unless you create a share link.
  • No, we can't touch your device. Burin Studio runs in a normal web page — it can't see your files, your Wi-Fi, your photos, or anything else on your phone or computer.
  • No hidden tracking. No ad networks, no selling your info, no analytics profiles following you around.

The full details are in the privacy policy.
