Burin Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

The short version: we don't sell your data, there are no ads, and there's no third-party tracking. We ask for your email only to sign you in. The audio and comments you add are yours, and stay private unless you create a share link.

What Burin Studio Does

Burin Studio lets you store audio (mixes, rehearsal tracks, demos), organize it into folders, and share it by a link so others can listen and leave feedback. It runs on the web and as Mac and iOS apps.

What We Collect

  • Your email address — to create your account and sign you in with a one-time code (no password).
  • The audio and content you add — tracks, folder names, comments, and locations. This is your content; we store it so the app works.
  • Basic technical logs — standard information (like device type and timestamps) needed to run and secure the service.

Listeners and commenters who open a link you shared do not need an account. If they leave a comment, we store the display name they type so their feedback is attributed — nothing more.

How We Use It

  • To provide the app: store and stream your audio, sync your library, and deliver the links you share.
  • To send you a sign-in code, and to email you when someone comments on your tracks (you can turn comment emails off).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell or rent your email or any of your data.
  • We do not show ads or use your content for advertising.
  • We do not run third-party tracking, ad networks, or profiling.
  • We cannot access your device — the app runs in a normal web page (or a sandboxed app) and has no access to your files, Wi-Fi, photos, or anything else on your device beyond what you choose to upload.

Sharing & Storage

Your content is private to your account unless you create a share link — then anyone with that link can access what you shared, by your choice. We use Cloudflare to store files and run the service. If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is handled by a payment processor (such as Apple or Stripe) — we never see your full card details.

Your Choices

You can delete tracks, folders, or your entire account at any time, which removes that content from our storage. To request deletion or ask a question, email support@hogbodylabs.com.

Children's Privacy

Burin Studio isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Email support@hogbodylabs.com
