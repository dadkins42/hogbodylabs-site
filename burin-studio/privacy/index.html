Privacy Policy

HogBody Labs · Last updated August 2026

Burin Studio (“the app”) is made by HogBody Labs. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address — to create your account and sign you in with a one-time code.
  • The audio and files you upload — your tracks, mixes, folder names, notes, and comments. This is your content; we store it so the app works.
  • Basic technical data — standard logs (device type, timestamps) needed to run and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: store and stream your audio, sync your library, and deliver the share links you create.
  • To secure the service and fix problems.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or third-party tracking.

Sharing

  • Your content is private to your account unless you create a share link. Anyone with a link you share can access that content, by your choice.
  • We use Cloudflare to store files and run the service. If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is handled by a payment processor (such as Apple or Stripe) — we never see your full card details.

Your choices

  • You can delete tracks, folders, or your entire account at any time, which removes that content from our storage.
  • To request deletion or ask a question, contact us at support@hogbodylabs.com.

Children

Burin Studio isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We’ll update this page if our practices change; the “last updated” date reflects the latest version.
